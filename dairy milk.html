About Dairy Milk

Cadbury Dairy Milk is a well-known milk chocolate brand loved by people around the world.
It was introduced by Cadbury in 1905 in the United Kingdom.
The chocolate is known for its rich, creamy texture and delicious taste.
It is made using milk, cocoa solids, sugar, and cocoa butter.
Over the years, it has become one of the most popular chocolates globally.
